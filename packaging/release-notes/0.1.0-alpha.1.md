The first test build of **Nebula Player**, a native video player for your
Jellyfin server and your own files, built on libcosmic and mpv. There's no
web view: every page is native, and mpv does the playing.

**This is an alpha.** It's for trying out, and some things are missing or
rough. Please report what breaks.

## What's in it

- **Sign in** with Quick Connect (approve a code from another Jellyfin app)
  or a username and password. Several servers and users can be saved, and
  you can switch between them in Settings.
- **Home**, laid out like Jellyfin's:
  - a featured banner that rotates through your movies and shows
    (Play / More Info, arrows and dots);
  - My Media;
  - Continue Watching and Next Up, where clicking a card plays it
    straight away;
  - Recently Added for each library.
- **Libraries** as poster grids, sorted by name, date added, release date
  or rating. Big libraries load more as you scroll.
- **Movie and show pages** with the backdrop, poster, details, Play or
  Resume, From the Start, mark watched, favorite, seasons with their
  episodes, cast and crew, and More Like This.
- **Search** across movies, shows and episodes.
- **Playback in mpv**, in its own window for now:
  - picks up where you left off;
  - uses your Jellyfin audio and subtitle language choices;
  - loads subtitle files stored next to the video;
  - plays the next episode by itself (you can turn that off);
  - keeps Jellyfin up to date, so your progress shows in other apps and
    on the server's dashboard.

  mpv's usual keys work. Your own `mpv.conf` and `input.conf` go in the
  folder Settings opens.
- **Local files**: add folders and play their videos. mpv remembers where
  you stopped. Nothing is scraped; titles and art come from Jellyfin only.
- **Jellyfin's dark look** with its purple-to-blue accent, or your
  desktop's own colors (Settings → Appearance).
- Hardware decoding is on by default, and full screen on start is a
  setting.

## Installing

- **Fedora:** `sudo dnf install ./nebula-player-*.rpm` (this pulls in
  `mpv-libs`).
- **Ubuntu / Debian:** `sudo apt install ./nebula-player_*.deb` (this pulls
  in `libmpv2`).
- **Other Linux:** unpack the `.tar.gz` and run `nebula-player`. libmpv
  needs to be installed.
- **Windows:** unzip and run `nebula-player.exe`. libmpv is included;
  keep `libmpv-2.dll` next to the exe.

## Known limits

- Video plays in mpv's own window. Showing it inside the app window comes
  later.
- Files are played as they are, never transcoded. mpv plays nearly
  everything, but a server set to force transcoding won't apply here.
- There's no in-app track picker yet. Use mpv's keys (`#` for audio, `j`
  for subtitles).
- Not tested on real hardware yet, including Windows.
