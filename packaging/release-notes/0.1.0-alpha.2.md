The second test build of **Nebula Player**. Video now plays inside the
app, with controls like Jellyfin Media Player's, and browsing feels
smoother.

**This is still an alpha.** Please report what breaks.

## New

- **Video plays in the app's window**, under its own controls:
  - a seek bar with the time played and the time left;
  - back 10 seconds and forward 30 seconds, play/pause, and previous and
    next episode;
  - volume and mute;
  - menus for the audio track, subtitles (or none) and playback speed;
  - full screen (double-click the video too);
  - a **Skip Intro** button (or Skip Credits, Recap, Preview) while one
    plays, when your server knows where they are.

  The controls fade away while the video plays and come back when you
  move the mouse. Click the video to pause. The keys are Jellyfin's:
  Space or K to pause, ← and → (or J and L) to skip back and forward, ↑
  and ↓ for volume, F for full screen, M to mute, S or Enter to skip an
  intro, Shift+N and Shift+P for the next and previous episode, 0–9 to
  jump through the video, and Esc to leave full screen, then the player.
- **Previews on the seek bar.** Hover over it, or drag it, to see the
  time, the chapter, and a thumbnail of that moment when your server has
  made them (Jellyfin 10.9+, with trickplay images turned on for the
  library). Each set of thumbnails is downloaded once, the first time
  it's needed, so this barely touches the server.
- **Libraries:**
  - the poster grid now fills the window's whole width;
  - an **A–Z bar** on the right jumps to a letter (when sorted by name);
  - a **search box** finds things in that library;
  - big libraries no longer stutter while scrolling, and you can jump or
    scroll anywhere without waiting for everything before it to load.
- **Smoother scrolling.** Mouse wheel steps glide instead of jumping, and
  a touchpad swipe keeps going after you lift your fingers.
- **Your desktop's accent color.** Buttons, switches and highlights take
  the accent color you picked in COSMIC (or KDE, or GNOME 47 and later).
  To keep Jellyfin's blue, go to Settings → Appearance → Accent color.
- **The sidebar** starts closed. Open it with the button at the top left;
  clicking anywhere else closes it.

## Changed

- To play in mpv's own window as before, with mpv's own controls and
  keys, turn on Settings → Play in mpv's own window. On a Mac, videos
  still always play in mpv's window.

## Installing

- **Fedora:** `sudo dnf install ./nebula-player-*.rpm` (this pulls in
  `mpv-libs`), or from COPR: `sudo dnf copr enable timbo-slice/nebula-player`
  then `sudo dnf install nebula-player`.
- **Ubuntu / Debian:** `sudo apt install ./nebula-player_*.deb` (this pulls
  in `libmpv2`).
- **Any Linux:** the `.flatpak` (`flatpak install --user
  NebulaPlayer-*.flatpak`) or the `.AppImage` (make it executable and run
  it). Both carry their own mpv.
- **Windows:** run the `-setup.exe` (no admin rights needed), or unzip
  the `.zip` and run `nebula-player.exe`. Keep `libmpv-2.dll` and the
  `vulkan` folder next to it.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** open the `.dmg` and drag Nebula Player to
  Applications. It isn't signed by Apple, so the first time, right-click
  it and choose Open. mpv comes inside the app.

## Known limits

- Files are played as they are, never transcoded. mpv plays nearly
  everything, but a server set to force transcoding won't apply here.
- On Windows, video inside the app is drawn without the graphics card's
  help, which may be heavy for 4K video. If it stutters, turn on Play in
  mpv's own window.
- Video inside the app hasn't been tried on NVIDIA graphics yet. If it
  doesn't show, use Play in mpv's own window and please report it.
- Windows, Mac and the Flatpak have still only been through the
  automatic build checks.
